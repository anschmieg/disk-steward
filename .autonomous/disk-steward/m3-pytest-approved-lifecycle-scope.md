# Approval-bound closed pytest corpus lifecycle

Continue from PR141 ca035ad with main as eventual integration baseline. Connect the closed two-record pytest corpus to a matching editable plan and the existing durable execution coordinator. Reuse the stage, restore, separately reviewed purge, approval ledger, owner lease, clock guard and uncertainty paths. Do not create another mutation engine or silently widen the five-file synthetic adapter.

The corpus plan has exactly two fixed cache records, Keep defaults, fixed copied-corpus evidence and a distinct database identity. Its frozen review is not an approval and remains non-executable on its own. The coordinator must explicitly select the corpus profile, use distinct persisted stores, create/reopen only that closed lifecycle profile, and bind its profile-specific contract and adapter versions into immutable manifests, preconditions and single-use approvals. Historical synthetic plans and manifests retain their existing format and meaning.

The purge adapter must match the explicit service, persisted control, plan and lifecycle profiles. Its corpus review kind and manifest adapter version differ from the synthetic contract. Cross-profile inputs, stale revisions, altered manifests, consumed approvals and uncertain outcomes refuse. Reopen reports known receipts and uncertainty without retrying work. Tests use only task-owned copies and trusted confirmation callbacks, preserving unselected records and unknown notes.

No renderer command, live inventory eligibility, project-file adoption or generic path mutation is added. This is integration needed before a real detector adapter can be validated; it is not live pytest ownership, idle-use, regeneration, native confirmation, controlled-volume recovery or M3 acceptance. The second generated family, duplicate execution and human/provider/credential/release gates remain open. Actual MVP completion precedes final adversarial review, fixes, validation and automation disable-stop.
